Questions we get on the phone

Straight answers

How long does it take?

A chip repair is about 30 minutes. A windshield replacement is usually two to three hours including cure time, and add roughly an hour if your car needs camera calibration. We'll give you the real number when we know the vehicle.

Can I drive right after a replacement?

Not immediately. Urethane needs to cure to hold the glass at crash strength, and the safe drive-away time depends on the adhesive and the weather that day. We tell you the exact time before you leave — it's a safety number, not a formality.

Does my car need ADAS calibration?

If it has lane keeping, adaptive cruise, or automatic emergency braking, almost certainly yes — those cameras look through the windshield and a new pane moves them off aim. We do it in-house on the same visit.

Will insurance cover it?

Comprehensive coverage typically includes glass. A chip repair often costs less than a deductible. Give us your policy or claim number and we'll check it for you and bill the carrier directly.

Do you come to me?

Yes, across Spartanburg County. Some jobs are better done in the shop — calibrations that need a level floor, or weather that stops urethane curing properly. We'll say which when you call.

What glass do you use?

Premium-grade laminated and tempered glass from established suppliers, meeting or exceeding federal safety standards. If you want OEM-branded glass specifically, ask and we'll price it.
