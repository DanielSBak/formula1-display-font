What comes to the shop

Why some jobs travel

If your car reads the road through the windshield — lane keeping, adaptive cruise, automatic braking — the camera behind the new glass has to be re-aimed to the manufacturer's spec. That needs a level floor, measured targets, and controlled light.

We do that in-house rather than sending you on to a dealer, so it's still one appointment. It just happens at Falling Creek Road instead of your driveway.

Straight answers

Boiling Springs, specifically

Is there a trip charge to come out here?

Boiling Springs is inside the normal mobile area, so it's quoted the same as work at the shop. If a job falls outside that, we say so on the phone before booking rather than adding it to the invoice afterwards.

Can you come to my workplace instead of home?

Yes — office lots and job sites are the usual case. We need the car parked somewhere reasonably level with room to open a door fully, and permission from whoever owns the lot.

How soon can you get here?

Depends on the day's route and whether your glass is in stock. Call and we'll tell you the honest next opening instead of booking you into one that slips.

Will insurance still cover a mobile visit?

Yes. Coverage follows the work, not the location. Give us your policy or claim number and we bill the carrier directly either way.
